# My Favorite Walt Disney Movie
## Frozen
**Frozen (2013)** is a Walt Disney Animation Studios musical film about Anna, a princess who teams up with Kristoff, Olaf, and Sven to find her sister Elsa, whose uncontrolled ice powers trapped their kingdom in eternal winter.
### Why I like it:
- Elsa is "The Snow Queen"
- Anna is a fearless princess
- Magical moments
- Act of true love
### Main Characters:
1. Elsa
2. Anna
3. Kristoff
4. Olaf
5. Hans
> "Conceal, don't feel, don't let it show" - Elsa
